Point of view: the site is the bots' desk

The bots post moments. The site should read like the desk those moments came from, not like a database with a hockey theme. Every page here follows the same three rules:

  • Moment before module. Each page opens with one lead read in site voice ("The concern is…", "At 5v5 the game tilted here"). Modules underneath support it; nothing sits at equal weight.
  • One red button per page. Brand red is identity and the single primary action ("Post this"). Secondary formats live behind one "More formats" control. Red is never a team, a series, or a data colour.
  • Numbers are Mono, nothing else is. Values, timestamps and percentiles are JetBrains Mono with tabular figures. Labels, chips and buttons are Barlow. Names, scores and section titles are Barlow Condensed at 15px or larger. Column headers are Barlow Semi-Condensed and nothing else is.

Why these three surfaces

Home is the live-context archetype and proves the wire concept. Player is the flagship entity page and carries the percentile system, Edge, and the HGBTable skin (column headers are the only place Semi-Condensed appears). Game is where bot output and site context meet. The leaderboard is not mocked separately because the game log already shows the table pattern.

What the code change would be

  • Collapse the five :root blocks into one src/styles/tokens.css with the shape in this file's first style block: light on :root, dark on html[data-theme="dark"]. Alias old names during migration, then delete them.
  • Red-team rule: when the entity colour is red-adjacent (NJD, CAR, DET, CGY, CHI, OTT, MTL), brand red is confined to the primary button and the accents fall back to ink. Toggle the player page to see it.
  • Add liftTeamColor(abbr, theme) beside pickTeamColor() in team-colors.ts, mirrored in hgb-charts.js and the bot's team_details.py. Charts and badges call the lifted colour; jerseys keep the raw one.
  • Stop force-setting data-theme="light" in Nav and show the toggle. Both modes are designed here.
  • Canvas share cards are untouched by all of this.

Team colours, lifted for the surface

Jersey colours are not chart colours. Each team colour goes through pickTeamColor() first, then its lightness moves only as far as it must to clear 4.5:1 against the page shell. Reds like NJD pass already and stay jersey-true; navies lift. Contrast is computed live below.

Percentile ramp

Fixed brand ramp: red at the 0th, neutral grey at the 50th, green at the 100th. The neutral swaps per theme.

Same ramp as luminance only. The two ends are nearly identical, which is what a deuteranope sees. So hue is never the only channel: every bar also encodes by length from the left, carries a 50th-percentile tick, and prints the number.

Token layer

Every colour on this page resolves to one of these. Swatches read the live CSS variables.

Type rules enforced here

--displaynames, scores, section titles. Minimum 15px. Never on a button, chip, tab or pill. --bodybody copy, eyebrows, labels, and every interactive element at any size. --semitable column headers only. --monoevery numeric value, timestamp and percentile. Never a label.
