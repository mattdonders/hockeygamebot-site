The sheet

A scoresheet is the most credible hockey document there is: paper, ink, ruled lines, numbers in columns, and nothing decorative. This pass sets the whole site that way. There are no cards, boxes, badges, tints or uppercase eyebrows anywhere. Structure comes from two things only: a margin column that names what each band is, and rules that separate bands. Everything else is type and whitespace.

What is different from the current site, on purpose

  • Sentence case headlines. Barlow Condensed at large sizes in sentence case reads like a sports section, not a broadcast graphic. Uppercase is kept for the small margin labels and column headers only.
  • No containers. A module is a band between two rules, labelled in the margin. Pages feel like one continuous document you can screenshot at any band and it still carries its label.
  • Red is scarce. It appears as the live pip, the wordmark, the one primary action per page ("Post the player card →"), and the low end of the percentile ramp. Buttons are text links with an arrow; filters are underlined words.
  • Team identity is a mark, not a badge. A 3px bar in the team's lifted colour sits before the name. Jersey colours are darkened only as far as needed to clear 4.5:1 on the paper; the table opposite is computed live.
  • Charts are ink. Win probability and xG are single ink lines with the paper showing through. Goals are dots in the team mark colour. No fills, no gradients, no second palette to learn.
  • Numbers get the size. The headline figures on a player page are Mono at 44px; names are Condensed. The old site made names huge and numbers small. Here the number is the argument.

Not in this pass

Dark mode (asked for light only; the same rules invert cleanly to ink paper). Canvas share cards, which keep their own system. Tables beyond the game log and linescore.

Team marks on paper

Type

  • --display names, scores, headlines, list names. Never below 15px, never interactive.
  • --body copy, margin labels, links, toggles, notes.
  • --semi column headers only.
  • --mono every number, time and percentile.
